Delivery

We deliver
across New Zealand

Enter your postcode to confirm delivery to your area.

Orders of 5kg+ qualify for home delivery nationwide. Dispatched Tue–Thu for weekend arrival.

How long does delivery take? +
Standard NZ Post perishable parcel delivery takes 2–4 business days. We dispatch Tuesday to Thursday so your order arrives fresh within the week.
What is the minimum order? +
Minimum order is 5kg, which qualifies for home delivery nationwide. This ensures every order arrives well-chilled and cost-effectively packed.
Do I need to be home for delivery? +
A signature is not required. Your order will be left in a safe, shaded spot. We recommend ensuring someone is home on the expected delivery day, especially in summer.
Can I substitute a cut if mine is unavailable? +
Yes — you can opt in to receive a similar cut if your selection is unavailable. You'll see a clear option at checkout to enable substitutions.
