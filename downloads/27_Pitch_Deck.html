The Challenges You Face

Your premium product deserves a premium customer experience. Currently, manual processes create friction that costs time, money, and customer satisfaction.

Complex Checkout

Customers struggle with weight minimums and variable pricing. Cart abandonment increases when the math is confusing.

Manual Inventory

Stock tracking via spreadsheets leads to overselling, stockouts, and hours of admin work each week.

No Order Visibility

Customers have no idea when their order is packed, shipped, or arriving — leading to support queries.

Hidden Value Proposition

Your unique story (on-farm abattoir, stress-free slaughter, 21-day ageing) isn't front and centre.

A Complete System Built for Juniper Hill

Modern Storefront

A beautiful, fast website that showcases your story and makes buying effortless. Pre-weighed packets with fixed prices — no calculator needed.

Smart Inventory

Scan QR labels to instantly add stock to your website. Real-time tracking means you never oversell or miss a sale.

Yield IQ

AI-powered yield prediction that learns from every carcass. Optimise cutting decisions based on demand and maximise returns.

Amplifying What Makes You Special

On-Farm Micro-Abattoir

NZ's first BioGro certified on-farm processing. Your website will make this the hero of your brand story.

Stress-Free Slaughter

No transport stress means lower cortisol, no "dark cutting", and superior meat quality. We'll communicate this clearly to customers.

21-Day Dry Ageing

Patient craft that develops incredible flavour. This premium process will be visible throughout the shopping experience.

Features That Drive Results

Packet-Based Inventory

Each cut is a unique packet with exact weight and price. No confusion, no minimum calculations.

Subscription Boxes

Recurring revenue from loyal customers with customisable delivery frequency and box contents.

Real-Time Order Tracking

Customers know exactly where their order is — from confirmed to delivered, with NZ Post integration.

Automated Email Updates

Professional emails at every stage keep customers informed and reduce "where's my order?" calls.

Mobile Scanner App

Staff scan QR codes to manage inventory on the go. No desktop required for day-to-day operations.

Unified Admin Portal

Orders, inventory, customers, subscriptions, labels, and analytics — all in one place.

Smart Discounts

First-purchase offers, bulk discounts, promo codes, and subscriber savings managed effortlessly.

AI Yield Prediction

Machine learning that improves with every carcass, helping butchers make optimal cutting decisions.
