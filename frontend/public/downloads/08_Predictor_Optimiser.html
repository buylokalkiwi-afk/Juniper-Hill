How Yield IQ Works

Yield IQ learns from every carcass you process.

When you enter a new carcass with its live weight and fat score, the AI predicts:

  • Expected aged weight — accounting for moisture loss during dry ageing
  • Saleable yield — how much meat you'll actually sell
  • Optimal cuts — based on current inventory and demand

The more carcasses you process, the smarter it gets. After 50+ animals, accuracy reaches 95%+.

INTERACTIVE

Visual Cut Selector

Click on each section of the cow to plan your cuts.

The interactive diagram shows:

  • Dark sections — not yet planned
  • Red sections — hover to see options
  • Green sections — cuts selected and priced

As you select cuts, the right panel updates with your running total. The AI suggests cuts based on what's selling well in your store.

OPTIMISED

Running Total & Optimization

See the projected revenue as you plan.

The running total shows:

  • Current planned revenue — from cuts you've selected
  • Comparison to average — are you above or below typical yield?
  • Suggestions — the AI highlights high-demand cuts you might be missing

Complete all sections, then click "Finalize Cuts" to send the plan to your butcher and add packets to inventory automatically.
