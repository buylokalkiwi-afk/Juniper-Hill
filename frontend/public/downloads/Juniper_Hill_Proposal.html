Why This Matters

Your premium product deserves a premium experience. This system eliminates checkout friction, provides real-time order visibility, and showcases what makes Juniper Hill special: stress-free on-farm processing, 100% organic practices, and 21-day dry ageing.

Current Challenges

1. Complex Checkout Experience

Customers currently struggle with weight minimums and variable pricing. The mental math required leads to cart abandonment and lost sales. Our solution: pre-weighed, pre-priced packets with exact costs displayed upfront.

2. Manual Inventory Management

Spreadsheet-based stock tracking leads to overselling (requiring refunds and apologies), stockouts, and hours of admin work each week. Our solution: real-time QR-based inventory that syncs automatically with the website.

3. No Order Visibility

Customers have no idea when their order is processed, packed, or shipped. This generates support queries and erodes confidence. Our solution: real-time order tracking with NZ Post integration and automated email updates at every stage.

4. Hidden Value Proposition

Juniper Hill's unique advantages — on-farm abattoir, stress-free slaughter, superior meat quality — aren't prominently communicated. Our solution: marketing-first design that leads with your story.

The Juniper Hill Difference

On-Farm Micro-Abattoir: NZ's first BioGro certified facility means zero transport stress, lower cortisol levels, and no "dark cutting" — resulting in more tender, flavourful beef that competitors simply cannot match.
